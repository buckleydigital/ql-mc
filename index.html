◈ QuoteLeadsAI

What it watches, and how it tells you
Alerts

Anything it spots always appears at the top of the QuoteLeadsAI panel, with a red count on the ◈ button until you have seen it. Switch on texts or calls below to have it reach you on your phone as well.

Your phone

He texts and rings from your main Twilio number (not set), set under Twilio settings, and only ever to the mobile above. Calls are for urgent items only (a proposal cold over 14 days, blocked fulfilment). On a call he speaks, then listens: answer him and he acts on it, and confirms in the panel (and by text if texts are on). Replying to his texts does not reach him, because replies to the main number go to the client agents; answer him in the panel instead.

Quiet hours hold a text or call until they end, rather than dropping it. The daily caps are the hard backstop. The main number needs the Voice capability for calls.

Voice in this browser

Saved on this device only, because which voices exist depends entirely on the phone or laptop you are on - a phone rarely has the same ones a desktop does. Auto prefers a British male voice and avoids the female ones; pick explicitly if it still gets it wrong.

Content brief

Read by him when he writes a post and by his editor when it checks one. The more specific, the less generic the posts.

Model

The model he thinks with, for the panel, texts and calls. Takes effect on your next question. Sonnet is cheaper and quick; Opus is better at long multi-step jobs like bulk sends and posts.
